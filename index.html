How Sound Maps to Data

Pitch Deeper earthquakes produce lower tones. A 600km deep quake sounds ~3 octaves below a surface event.
Volume & Duration Magnitude controls loudness and how long the voice sustains. M6+ events bloom for up to 5 minutes.
Stereo Position Events east of you pan right, west pan left. Longitude difference determines placement.
Reverb Distance Distant earthquakes sound further away with more reverb. Nearby events feel more present.
Background Drone The constant pad responds to overall activity. Higher average magnitude = brighter tone.
